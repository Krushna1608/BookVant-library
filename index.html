About BookVant Project

Our Objective

BookVant provides quick, seamless digital access to Maharashtra State Board curriculum textbooks from Class 1 to Class 12.

Fast & Lightweight Experience

Designed for slow internet speeds and low device storage by rendering PDFs directly online.

Stream Separation

Class 11th & 12th are streamlined into Science, Commerce, and Arts streams with direct English Medium textbook links.

Help & Support

1. How to Access Books? (पुस्तके कशी पाहावीत?)

Go to Student Login, register your email ID, and log in. Choose your Class or Stream to read PDFs directly.

2. Email ID & Password Issues

Ensure you enter a valid Email ID (e.g., student@gmail.com). Use the Forgot Password? option if needed.

3. Class 11th & 12th Stream Selection

For Junior College (Std 11 & 12), select your stream (Science / Commerce / Arts) to view English medium textbooks directly.
